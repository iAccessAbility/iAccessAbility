Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 30, 2026

iAccessAbility wishes to respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information Collected

No personal information is collected and recorded unless you reach out directly or submit information into a form on the iAccessAbility website.

  • Your name:: Full first name and last name initial at minimum are required when you contact iAccessAbility. You may also provide a prefered name.
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your device details (model, software version, etc.)
  • Any other information you include in your message

2. How We Use Collected Information

Any information you provide is used only to:

  • Respond to your inquiries or messages personally
  • Improve our services and content
  • Properly quote you if and when you submit any public reviews. Reviews submitted are stored in a Google Sheet and are automatically published to the reviews page.

3. How We Store Information

Your name and contact information are stored securely in Google Contacts under the iAccessAbility account and are kept until you request deletion. iAccessAbility utilizes Zoho Mail for all email interations and emails are typically discarded automatically after 30 days. See also Zoho's Privacy Polocy We do not sell or freely share your personal information with third parties.

We strongly encourage you to review their privacy policies separately. iAccessAbility also utilizes various platforms such as Google products and Zoho products and may use such services to store data and information we collect about you. Your interactions with the iAccessAbility social media pages are held under the platform's polocies and are not recorded elsewhere.
